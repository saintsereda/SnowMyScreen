A few things you might be wondering

Support ↗
What is Wonderfall?

Wonderfall is a macOS menu bar app for animated desktop overlays: snow, rain, autumn leaves, spring petals, summer fireflies, custom PNG artwork and decorative lights.

Is Wonderfall free?

Wonderfall is a paid download, listed at US$1.99 in the US Mac App Store. Prices vary by region and may change; check your local store for the current price.

Is it a live wallpaper or a screensaver?

It is a desktop overlay. Your existing wallpaper stays in place, and the animation can appear behind or over open windows while you use your Mac. It does not replace the macOS screensaver.

Does it work with multiple displays?

Yes. The app creates an overlay for each connected screen and updates the overlays when the display setup changes.

Can I still click my apps and desktop?

Yes. The overlay windows ignore mouse events, so you can click through them and use your Mac normally. Desktop only mode places the effects behind your windows.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Import up to 10 PNG images, each up to 5 MB and 1024 × 1024 pixels. Choose one or mix several, with snow-like, leaf-like or rain-like motion.

Can I add Christmas lights without snowfall?

Yes. Garlands have separate controls from weather effects, so you can light up the top menu bar without snowfall.

Does the rain effect include sound?

The rain effect is a visual desktop overlay. It does not include ambient rain audio or music.

Which Macs are supported?

Wonderfall requires macOS 14.6 or later. This is a Mac app; it is not an iPhone or iPad app.

Does it need an internet connection?

Desktop effects, settings and custom PNGs work locally on your Mac. Downloading and updating require internet access. Optional weather matching also needs internet and location permission to use Apple WeatherKit.

How do I turn the effects off?

Use the menu bar controls to disable weather effects or lights, or quit the app. Launch-at-login behavior is a separate setting.

Desktop effects for Mac. Your wallpaper stays

Wonderfall adds animated snow, rain, autumn leaves, spring petals and fireflies to your Mac desktop. Keep the effects behind your windows or let them drift over your apps, with your own wallpaper underneath.
