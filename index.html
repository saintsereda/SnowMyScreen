Questions, answered

A few things you might be wondering

Support ↗
What is Wonderfall?

Wonderfall is a macOS menu bar app for animated desktop overlays. The current App Store release, SnowMyScreen 1.1.2, includes falling snow and custom PNG images. The upcoming seasonal update adds rain, autumn leaves, spring petals, summer fireflies and decorative lights.

Why does the App Store say SnowMyScreen?

Wonderfall is the new website and product name for the same app by Andrii Sereda. Until the next store update, the download is listed as SnowMyScreen 1.1.2. The App Store app ID is unchanged.

Is Wonderfall free?

The current SnowMyScreen release is a paid download, listed at US$1.99 in the US Mac App Store. Prices vary by region and may change; check your local store for the current price.

Is it a live wallpaper or a screensaver?

It is a desktop overlay. Your existing wallpaper stays in place, and the animation can appear behind or over open windows while you use your Mac. It does not replace the macOS screensaver.

Does it work with multiple displays?

Yes. The app creates an overlay for each connected screen and updates the overlays when the display setup changes.

Can I still click my apps and desktop?

Yes. The overlay windows ignore mouse events, so you can click through them and use your Mac normally. Desktop only mode places the effects behind your windows.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Import up to 10 PNG images, each up to 5 MB and 1024 × 1024 pixels. Choose one or mix several as falling shapes. The upcoming update adds leaf-like and rain-like motion for custom artwork.

Can I add Christmas lights without snowfall?

Garlands are part of the upcoming seasonal update previewed on this page. They have separate controls from weather effects, so you can decorate the menu bar, Dock or both without snowfall.

Does the rain effect include sound?

The upcoming rain effect is a visual desktop overlay. It does not include ambient rain audio or music.

Which Macs are supported?

Wonderfall requires macOS 14.6 or later. Check the available Mac App Store release before downloading. This is a Mac app; it is not an iPhone or iPad app.

Does it need an internet connection?

Snowfall, settings and custom PNGs work locally on your Mac. Downloading and updating require internet access. Optional weather matching in the upcoming update also needs internet and location permission to use Apple WeatherKit.

How do I turn the effects off?

Use the menu bar controls to disable weather effects or lights, or quit the app. Launch-at-login behavior is a separate setting.
