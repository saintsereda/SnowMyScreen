Questions, answered

A few things you might be wondering.

Support ↗
What is SnowMyScreen?

SnowMyScreen is a macOS menu bar app that adds animated effects to your desktop: snow, rain, autumn leaves, spring petals, summer fireflies and custom PNG images. It also includes decorative lights for the menu bar and Dock.

Is it a live wallpaper or a screensaver?

It is a desktop overlay. Your existing wallpaper stays in place, and the animation can appear behind or over open windows while you use your Mac. It does not replace the macOS screensaver.

Does it work with multiple displays?

Yes. The app creates an overlay for each connected screen and updates the overlays when the display setup changes.

Can I still click my apps and desktop?

Yes. The overlay windows ignore mouse events, so you can click through them and use your Mac normally. Desktop only mode places the effects behind your windows.

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Import up to 10 PNG images, each up to 5 MB and 1024 × 1024 pixels. Choose one or mix several. Custom images can move like snow, leaves or rain.

Can I add Christmas lights without snowfall?

Yes. Weather effects and garlands have separate controls. Put lights on the menu bar, Dock or both, and choose a palette, shape and animation.

Does the rain effect include sound?

The current app provides visual effects. It does not include rain audio, music, weather forecasts or a live weather feed.

Which Macs are supported?

The app target requires macOS 14.6 or later. Check the current Mac App Store listing for the requirements of the available release before downloading. It is a Mac app, not an iPhone or iPad app.

Does it need an internet connection?

Effects, settings and custom images work locally on your Mac. Downloading or updating through the Mac App Store requires internet access.

How do I turn the effects off?

Use the menu bar controls to disable weather effects or lights, or quit the app. Launch-at-login behavior is a separate setting.
