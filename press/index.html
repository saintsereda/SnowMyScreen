About Wonderfall

Wonderfall brings snow, rain, falling leaves, petals, fireflies and string lights to your Mac desktop, over your own wallpaper.

Snow, leaves and petals pile up at the bottom of the screen, and raindrops form on the glass. A shovel, broom or squeegee clears them away. Clicks pass through the effects, so you keep working normally.

The story

Two winters ago it didn't snow in Kraków at all. Andrew Sereda missed it and wrote a tiny utility that let snow fall along the top of the screen, just to lift the mood. It was never meant to go anywhere, until someone on a work call noticed the snow and asked where they could get one. So it got finished and went on the Mac App Store as SnowMyScreen.

The next ideas came from nostalgia for the PC games Andrew played as a kid. Snow started piling up at the bottom of the screen, and then it needed a shovel. Version 1.2 adds rain, leaves, petals and fireflies, and a new name: Wonderfall.

What's new in 1.2

  • Rain, autumn leaves, flower petals and fireflies alongside snow
  • Snow banks, raindrops and leaf and petal piles you clear with a shovel, squeegee or broom
  • String lights along the menu bar, with winter, Halloween, New Year and holiday scenes
  • Your own images, including SVG, as falling shapes
  • Optional scenes that match your local weather
  • A new name and icon

From the developer

The name is about the small magic of the seasons: “wonder” for that feeling, “fall” for things that fall.

Andrew Sereda, developer of Wonderfall
