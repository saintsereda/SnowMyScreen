Wonderfall Support & Setup for Mac

Use the menu bar icon to choose a seasonal effect, adjust its motion, add garlands or import your own PNG images.

Getting started

Download Wonderfall from the Mac App Store, open the app and click its menu bar icon to choose a theme in Weather effects. The settings window does not need to stay open.

I cannot see an effect

Check that Weather effects is enabled and the app is running. Try Full screen and Over the content placement. Desktop only effects sit behind your windows, so a maximized window can hide them. Increase intensity if the effect is too subtle.

Importing custom artwork

Import a PNG through the custom image controls. The limit is 10 images, each up to 5 MB and 1024 × 1024 pixels. Transparent backgrounds give clean edges. Choose snow-like, leaf-like or rain-like motion.

Controlling lights separately

Garland controls let you decorate the top menu bar. Lights and weather effects work independently, so lights can be used on their own.

Turning off automatic launch

Use the launch-at-login setting to choose whether the app opens when you sign in. Quit the app from its menu bar controls.

Contact the developer

Email hello@andrewsereda.com with your macOS version, app version, display setup and the steps that reproduce the problem. Avoid attaching personal or sensitive content.
