Penguins on Your Mac Desktop: Call a Penguin Parade Across the Snow

Call three penguins onto your Mac desktop with Wonderfall: they run in, belly-slide across the snow, step up onto the Dock, wave and run off by themselves.

Get on Mac App Store

To get penguins on your Mac desktop, install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store, click its icon in the menu bar and choose Call Penguins. Three penguins run onto the display under your pointer, belly-slide across the snow along the bottom, brake, stand up, wave and run off by themselves. Turn on Snow accumulation first, and they slide along the snow bank that builds up on your screen.

Wonderfall is a menu bar app that draws weather and winter decorations on transparent layers over your desktop, so your wallpaper stays exactly as it is and the penguins pass in front of it. They arrived in Wonderfall 1.6 on November 19, 2026. This guide shows how to call them, how to give them a snow bank and the Dock to climb, and what they don’t do.

How do you get penguins on your Mac desktop?

Choose Call Penguins in Wonderfall’s menu bar menu, and three penguins come onto the display under your pointer.

  1. Install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store. It needs macOS 14.6 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  2. Give them some snow (optional). Choose Snow in the Season menu, then open Settings…, go to Surface and tools and turn on Snow accumulation. Wait while a bank builds up along the bottom of the screen.
  3. Point at the right screen. With more than one display, move the pointer to the one where you want the penguins.
  4. Click the Wonderfall icon in the menu bar and choose Call Penguins. You can also open Decoration Inventory from the same menu and click Call Penguins next to Penguins.

If you can’t choose Call Penguins, check that Pause all effects is off and that effects are turned on for at least one display in Settings > General > Displays.

What do the penguins do on your screen?

Three penguins run in from the side of the screen, crouch and dive onto their bellies, slide across the snow, brake, stand up, wave and run off, then leave on their own.

Every call is one pass. Call again, and a new pass starts on that display; there are never more than three penguins on it at once. Each call switches the direction, so if one group came in from the left, the next comes from the right.

The penguins don’t make a sound. In Wonderfall, only the cleaning tools, snow play and Santa’s optional sleigh sound make any noise.

Do the penguins need snow, and can they climb the Dock?

They don’t need snow, but they look best with it: with Snow accumulation on, they run and slide on top of the snow bank, and with the Dock at the bottom of the screen they step up onto it.

Without a snow bank, the penguins walk along the bottom edge of the screen. The bank needs the Full screen weather area, which Snow accumulation switches on for you, and a Mac that runs Wonderfall’s Metal renderer. The faster the snow falls, the faster the bank grows, so a higher snow intensity gets you there sooner.

If your Dock sits on the left or right, move it to the bottom in System Settings > Desktop & Dock, where Apple lets you place the Dock on the left, bottom or right edge, and the next group will step up onto it. When the drift gets too deep, pick up the Shovel from the Wonderfall menu or press Control-Option-S and dig, or choose Cleanup time! to send a small snowplow across the screen.

Is there a penguin desktop pet for Mac?

Wonderfall’s penguins are visitors rather than a desktop pet: they come when you call them, cross the screen and leave, and they don’t stay on your desktop or react to clicks.

There’s no schedule either. Santa flies past every so often by himself, but the penguins never turn up uninvited, so they won’t pop up in the middle of a screen share. What you get is a short show whenever you want one, as often as you like.

With Reduce motion turned on in System Settings > Accessibility > Motion, the penguins don’t run or slide. They appear standing still for about five seconds and then disappear.

Will the penguins get in the way of your work?

No: the penguins ignore clicks, so everything under them keeps working, and you choose whether they pass in front of your windows or behind them.

They follow the Weather Display layer in Settings > Weather, the same layer as the snow. You can also switch it from the Weather display submenu in the Wonderfall menu. Based on Wonderfall 1.6, checked on November 24, 2026:

What else goes with penguins on a winter desktop?

The penguins share the snow with the rest of Wonderfall’s winter scene: a snowman you build and dress, Christmas trees, Santa’s flyover and lights along the menu bar.

  • A snowman. Choose Play in the snow, roll three snowballs and stack them. Right-click the snowman while you play, or click it once you’ve stopped, to dress it in the Snowman Wardrobe. The snowman guide walks you through it.
  • Christmas trees. Check Christmas tree in the Wonderfall menu, and place more from Decoration Inventory, the same window that calls the penguins. The Christmas tree guide shows how to decorate them.
  • Santa. Set Santa flyover to Sometimes or Often in Settings > Holiday, or choose Call Santa now.
  • Lights. Turn on Light Strip in Settings > Lights and pick the Winter Night scene. The Christmas lights guide covers palettes and animations.

The penguins, the Snowman Wardrobe and extra trees all arrived together; the Wonderfall 1.6 release post covers them side by side.

Common questions

Do the penguins come by themselves?

No. They come only when you choose Call Penguins, and they leave on their own after one pass. Santa is the only visitor with a schedule.

Can I click or drag the penguins?

No. They ignore clicks, which pass through to whatever is underneath, so they never block a button or an icon.

Can I have more than three penguins?

Not on one display at the same time. Calling again starts a new pass of three on that display, coming from the other side.

Do I need snow for the penguins?

No. Without a snow bank they walk along the bottom of the screen. With Snow accumulation on, they slide along the top of the bank.

Which version of Wonderfall has penguins?

Version 1.6 and later. If you already have Wonderfall, update it from the Updates section of the App Store on your Mac.

Is there a Windows or iPhone version?

No. Wonderfall is a Mac app only; it doesn’t run on Windows, iPhone or iPad.

Where should you start?

Get Wonderfall on the Mac App Store (a one-time US$3.99 in the US), choose Snow, turn on Snow accumulation and let the bank build up. Then choose Call Penguins and watch three penguins belly-slide across your desktop and step up onto the Dock. For the rest of the winter scene, follow the guide to a Christmas theme for your Mac.
