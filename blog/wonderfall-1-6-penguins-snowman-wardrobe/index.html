Wonderfall 1.6: Penguins, a Snowman Wardrobe and More Christmas Trees

Wonderfall 1.6 brings penguins to your Mac desktop, a wardrobe to dress up your snowman, and more Christmas trees you decorate in place.

Get on Mac App Store

Wonderfall 1.6 is a winter update to the Mac desktop effects app, out on the Mac App Store since November 19, 2026. Penguins now waddle and slide across the snow on your screen, the snowmen you build can be dressed in a hat, face and clothes, and you can place more Christmas trees on your desktop and decorate each one in place.

Version 1.5 let you decorate the Christmas tree and choose how your lights glow. Version 1.6 brings new friends to your winter desktop and more room to make it your own. If you already have Wonderfall, it arrives as a regular update in the App Store. Version 1.6.1 has since made the characters 3D and lets trees and snowmen ride on your windows.

In this updatePenguinsSnowman WardrobeMore Christmas treesEverything elseHow to update

Penguins on your desktop

Choose Call Penguins in the Wonderfall menu in the menu bar, and a little group of penguins waddles out and slides across the snow at the bottom of your screen.

Dress up your snowman

Build a snowman in snow play, then click it to open the Snowman Wardrobe and pick a hat, a face and clothes. A top hat or a trapper hat, a red or a plaid scarf: every snowman can look different. The snowman guide shows how to build one.

More Christmas trees, decorated in place

Open Decoration Inventory from the Wonderfall menu to place more Christmas trees on your desktop. Right-click any tree to decorate it right where it stands, without going to Settings. Version 1.5 explains the toppers, ornaments and colors.

Everything else in 1.6

  • Rolling and lifting snowballs in snow play is more reliable.

How do you get Wonderfall 1.6?

If you already have Wonderfall, open the App Store on your Mac, click Updates and update Wonderfall. If not, get it on the Mac App Store; it needs macOS 14.6 or later. Then open the Wonderfall menu in the menu bar and call the penguins.
