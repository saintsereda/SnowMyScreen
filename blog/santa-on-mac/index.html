Santa on Your Mac: Call His Sleigh Across the Desktop, Now in 3D

How to get Santa and his reindeer flying across your Mac desktop: turn on Santa flyover, call him any time, make him 3D, and see other Mac apps with a Santa.

Get on Mac App Store

To get Santa on your Mac screen, install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store and set Santa flyover in Settings > Holiday to Sometimes or Often: Santa and his reindeer then fly across your desktop every so often while you work, with a sleigh sound you can turn off. Choose Call Santa now in the menu bar to send him right away; since version 1.6.1 he can be drawn in 3D.

Santa is the one Christmas visitor nobody has to set up twice: once he’s on, he turns up by himself. This guide shows how to switch him on in Wonderfall, how often he flies, how to make him 3D or silent, and which other Mac apps have a Santa of their own. Wonderfall is our app; facts about the other apps come from their own App Store listings or websites, checked on November 28, 2026.

How do you get Santa on your Mac screen?

Santa comes with Wonderfall, a menu bar app from the Mac App Store: install it, and Santa’s flyover is set to Sometimes from the start, so he crosses your screen on his own every so often.

  1. Install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store. It needs macOS 14.6 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  2. Open the Holiday settings. Click the Wonderfall icon in the menu bar, choose Settings… and open Holiday.
  3. Pick how often. Under Santa flyover, set the frequency to Sometimes or Often.
  4. Call him now. Click Call Santa now to see him straight away. The same command is in the Wonderfall menu in the menu bar.

How often does Santa fly across the screen?

Santa flies roughly every 10 to 25 minutes on Sometimes, the default, and roughly every 3 to 8 minutes on Often; Off stops the flights.

The exact moment varies, so he still feels like a surprise. Wonderfall also picks the direction of each flight and, with more than one display, which display he crosses. If you don’t want to wait, Call Santa now sends him across right away, whatever the frequency is set to.

Can Santa be 3D on a Mac?

Yes: since Wonderfall 1.6.1, Santa and his reindeer can be drawn in 3D; choose 3D under Character style in Settings > General.

The same switch changes the penguins, snowmen, Christmas trees, the tools and the cleaning vehicles at once, so the whole winter scene matches. New installs start with 3D; if you updated from version 1.6, you keep the pixel-art Santa until you switch. The flight itself was reworked in 1.6.1 as well. Here is everything new in 1.6.1.

Does Santa make a sound?

Santa’s sleigh makes a sound as he flies past, and it is on by default; turn off Santa sound in Settings > Holiday for a silent Santa.

Apart from Santa’s sleigh, only the hand tools and snow play make a sound in Wonderfall. Snow, lights and the other effects are silent.

Will Santa get in the way of your work?

Santa flies over your windows but never blocks them: all of Wonderfall’s overlays ignore mouse clicks, so every click goes through to the app underneath, and a flight lasts only as long as it takes him to cross the screen.

  • One display only. In Settings > General > Displays, turn effects off for a display, and Santa won’t fly there.
  • Reduce Motion. When Reduce motion is on in System Settings > Accessibility > Motion, Santa doesn’t fly at all.
  • A quiet moment. Before a call or a screen share, Pause all effects in the Wonderfall menu pauses everything.

Is there a Santa app for Mac?

Yes, several Mac apps have a Santa: in Wonderfall he flies across the screen by himself or when you call him, and on November 28, 2026, Snow Now, Warm Mittens, Festive Desk and iSnow listed a Santa too, as did KoiArt and CozyMac, which are sold on their own websites.

Wonderfall’s Santa doesn’t stop or bring presents: he is a flyover, and the app puts its effort into making him part of a whole winter desktop, with snow that piles up, lights, trees and penguins. For more holiday apps side by side, see our comparison of Christmas apps for Mac.

Can Santa stay on your screen?

No: in Wonderfall, Santa always flies past and leaves; for decorations that stay, place a Christmas tree or build a snowman.

Christmas trees from Decoration Inventory stand at the bottom of the screen or on top of a window until you remove them, and a snowman you build stands in the snow. Penguins visit only when you choose Call Penguins, and leave by themselves. Wonderfall has no advent calendar, no daily gifts and no countdown, so Santa brings the mood, not presents. The Christmas tree guide shows how to place and decorate trees.

What goes with Santa on a Christmas desktop?

Santa looks best with the rest of Wonderfall’s winter scene: snow that piles up, lights along the menu bar, a Christmas tree and penguins sliding through.

  • Snow. In Settings > Weather, choose Snow, and turn on Snow accumulation in Surface and tools so it piles up on your windows and along the bottom of the screen.
  • Lights. In Lights, turn on Light Strip and pick Holiday Lights. The lights guide covers every setting.
  • Penguins. Choose Call Penguins, and three penguins run in, belly-slide across the snow, wave and run off. More about the penguins.

Where should you start?

Get Wonderfall on the Mac App Store (US$3.99 once in the US), open Settings > General and pick 3D under Character style, then click Call Santa now in Holiday and watch his sleigh cross your desktop.
