Wonderfall 1.5: Decorate Your Own Christmas Tree, Lights That Sway in the Wind and New Cleaning Vehicles

Wonderfall 1.5 lets you decorate the Christmas tree on your Mac desktop, makes the lights sway in the wind and lets you pick the vehicle that clears the snow.

Get on Mac App Store

Wonderfall 1.5 is the holiday update to the Mac desktop effects app, out on the Mac App Store since November 16, 2026. You can now decorate the Christmas tree on your desktop with a topper, ornaments and ornament colors, let the menu bar lights sway in the wind of the weather on your screen or glow in your own colors, and pick the vehicle that clears the snow.

Version 1.4 put Santa and a Christmas tree on your screen. Version 1.5 lets you make that holiday desktop your own: you choose how the tree is decorated, which colors the lights glow in and which little vehicle drives out to clean up. If you already have Wonderfall, it arrives as a regular update in the App Store.

What’s new in Wonderfall 1.5?

Wonderfall 1.5 adds a Christmas tree you decorate yourself, menu bar lights that can sway in the wind, use your own colors and change style for four holidays, and a choice of vehicles that clear snow, leaves and petals. The table compares it with 1.4, based on the release notes and the app’s settings, checked on November 18, 2026.

Can you decorate the Christmas tree in Wonderfall?

Yes. Since version 1.5 you choose the Christmas tree’s style, topper, ornaments and ornament colors in Settings, and the tree on your desktop changes as soon as you click.

  1. Click the Wonderfall icon in the menu bar and open Settings.
  2. Open Holiday, keep Christmas tree on and choose its size: Small, Medium or Large.
  3. Pick a Tree style for a ready-made look in one click: Classic (star, baubles, red and gold), Winter (snowflake, baubles, blue and silver), Cozy (bow, sweets, colorful) or Minimal (no topper and no ornaments).
  4. Or mix your own: set Tree topper to Star, Snowflake, Bow or None, Ornaments to Baubles, Sweets, Wooden ornaments or None, and Ornament colors to Red and gold, Blue and silver or Colorful.

Out of the box the tree has a star and no ornaments, so no style tile is selected until you pick one. Ornament colors come in those three sets: there is no free color picker for the tree, and the colors can’t be chosen while Ornaments is set to None. The tree’s own lights still follow the palette and animation of your menu bar lights. You can drag the tree sideways along the bottom of the screen, and it keeps clear of the Dock.

Can Wonderfall lights use your own colors?

Yes. With Use custom colors on, the menu bar lights glow in one to six colors you choose yourself instead of one of the 12 built-in palettes.

  1. In Settings, open Lights.
  2. Next to Color palette, click Customize and turn on Use custom colors. It starts from the colors of the palette you had.
  3. Pick between one and six colors with the standard macOS color picker, the same Colors window that other Mac apps use.

Choosing a scene turns custom colors off, so pick the scene first and change the colors after. Custom colors apply to the string along the menu bar only, not to the Christmas tree’s lights.

Do the lights sway in the wind?

They can. With Sway with weather on, the menu bar lights swing in the wind of the weather effect on your screen, following its direction and strength.

The switch is in Lights, in the Light & motion section, and it’s off until you turn it on. The Wind sensitivity slider sets how much the lights move, from 0 to 100% (65% by default). They sway only while a weather effect is running and macOS Reduce motion is off. The wind comes from the app’s falling snow, rain, leaves or petals, not from the weather outside your window.

Do the lights change for the holidays?

Yes, if you turn on Switch light styles for holidays: on Valentine’s Day, Halloween, Christmas and New Year the lights switch to a matching palette and bulb shape, then switch back by themselves when the holiday ends.

You’ll find it in Lights, under Holiday Calendar, and each holiday can be turned on or off separately. The dates follow your Mac’s local time, first and last day included:

HolidayDatesPaletteBulb shape
Valentine’s DayFebruary 7–14SakuraHeart
HalloweenOctober 24–31Haunted GlowPumpkin lantern

Only the palette and the bulb shape change. Animation, brightness, bulb size and spacing stay as you set them, and your own palette and shape are kept for when the holiday is over. If you change the scene, palette, shape or custom colors during a holiday, the holiday style pauses until that holiday ends. Turn it on now, keep Christmas selected, and the Christmas style starts by itself on December 1. The lights still hang along the menu bar only; the Christmas lights guide shows how to set them up.

Can you choose the vehicle that clears the snow?

Yes. In 1.5 you pick which small vehicle drives across the bottom of the screen when you choose Cleanup time!, with separate choices for snow, leaves and petals.

  1. Turn on a surface effect in Surface and tools: Snow accumulation, Leaf piles or Petal piles, so there is something to clean.
  2. In the same section, under Cleaning vehicles, click the picture of the vehicle you like. Only the choices for the weather you’re using are shown.
  3. Choose Cleanup time! in the Wonderfall menu in the menu bar, or click the button in Settings, and watch your vehicle clear the screen.

There are four snow vehicles: the classic tractor-style snowplow, plus a pickup, a tracked one and a rotary one. Leaves have four too: the classic sweeper, plus vacuum, roller and rake styles. Petals now have five of their own: a conveyor-style vehicle, plus roller, tracked, truck and sweeper styles. The tiles show pictures without names, and the vehicles drive silently. Rain has no vehicle: Cleanup time! simply wipes the droplets away, and the squeegee is still there for wiping by hand, like the shovel and the broom. Cleaning needs the Full screen weather area and Wonderfall’s Metal renderer; the snowman guide explains how the snow bank works.

What else changed in 1.5?

Version 1.5 also refreshes the pixel art of the holiday decorations and cleaning tools, and fixes a rare freeze when Santa’s sound started.

  • New pixel artwork for the tree, its decorations and the cleaning tools.
  • Three new sliders for the shape of the lights: Segment width, Wire sag and Hanging length. Open Lights and click Customize next to Shape.
  • A fix for a rare freeze when Santa’s sleigh sound started.

Santa’s flyover, the tool rack, snow play and the keyboard shortcuts from 1.4 work as before. Here’s what 1.4 added.

How do you get Wonderfall 1.5?

If you already have Wonderfall, update it in the App Store; if not, download it from the Mac App Store, where it costs US$3.99 once in the US and needs macOS 14.6 or later.

  1. Open the App Store on your Mac and click Updates in the sidebar (Apple’s guide to updating apps).
  2. Click Update next to Wonderfall.
  3. Open Wonderfall’s Settings from the menu bar, go to Holiday and give your tree some ornaments.

Start with the tree: pick Cozy or Winter, then turn on Switch light styles for holidays so the lights change for Christmas on their own. See how Wonderfall compares with other Christmas apps for Mac, or get Wonderfall on the Mac App Store and decorate your desktop tonight.
