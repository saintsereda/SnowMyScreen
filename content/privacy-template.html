Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Introduction

Wonderfall adds seasonal desktop effects and decorative lights. This policy describes the app and website.

The Short Version

Your settings and imported images stay on your device. Weather matching is optional.

Optional weather matching uses location access and Apple WeatherKit. Website analytics require a separate choice.

Privacy in the Desktop App

  • No account registration
  • No in-app analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No upload of settings or imported images
  • No desktop screenshots or screen recordings
  • No microphone or camera access
  • No location history stored by the app

What Stays on Your Mac

The app saves your preferences locally on your device:

  • Snow intensity and speed
  • Wind direction and strength
  • Snowflake shapes and colors
  • Which displays show snow
  • Language preference

Settings and images stay local. Resetting preferences preserves imported images; removing the app does not necessarily remove saved data.

Optional Location and Weather Matching

Weather matching is off by default. With your permission, location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to choose a decorative effect.

The app uses location in memory and does not save a location history. Weather normally refreshes every 30 minutes while enabled.

You can turn matching off or revoke location access. Apple processes weather requests under its own privacy policies.

Apple Weather & Privacy · Apple Privacy Policy

Permissions

Location and launch at login are optional. You choose imported PNG files through the system file picker. Effects do not require screen recording, microphone or camera access.

Offline Effects and Online Services

Manual effects work offline. Weather matching, attribution, external links and App Store updates use the internet.

Updates

Apple handles purchases, updates and any system diagnostics you choose to share. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Children

The app has no accounts, advertising or in-app analytics. Optional location access uses system permissions for all users.

Website analytics & cookies
