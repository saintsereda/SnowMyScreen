# Wonderfall

> Animated desktop effects for Mac by Andrii (Andrew) Sereda. The Mac App Store app ID is 6755106853. Wonderfall was formerly named SnowMyScreen; both names refer to the same app.

## Wonderfall for Mac
- Requires macOS {minimum_os} or later; Mac only.
- Seasonal effects: falling snow, rain, autumn leaves, spring petals and summer fireflies.
- Custom images (common formats and SVG) can move like snow, leaves or rain. Import up to 10 at once; the app prepares the size automatically.
- Version 1.3 (November 2026) is the current App Store release. Snow settles on top of open windows, the snow bank can be rolled into snowballs and a snowman, and a snowplow clears it. Decorative lights with eight scenes hang along the menu bar, the Dock or both. Interactive tools clear snow, leaves, petals and raindrops.
- Place the overlay over open windows or on the desktop behind them, while keeping the existing wallpaper.
- Controls include intensity, size, speed and wind. Snow can pile up at the bottom of the screen and be cleared with a shovel.
- Optional weather matching uses location permission and Apple WeatherKit.
- Rain is a visual effect without ambient rain audio.
- US store price checked on {checked_at}: {us_price}. Prices depend on the region and may change; the App Store is the source of pricing.
- The app is a desktop overlay, not a replacement for wallpaper or the macOS screensaver.
