To build a snowman on your Mac desktop, install Wonderfall, choose Snow and turn on Snow accumulation so snow piles up along the bottom of the screen. Then choose Play in the snow in the menu bar: drag along the snow to roll a snowball and stack three balls into a snowman. Right-click it to dress it in the Snowman Wardrobe, or lift it onto the top of a window.

Falling snow is nice to look at. Snow you can play with is better. Wonderfall turns the snow bank at the bottom of your screen into something you can scoop up, roll and build with, and every snowman you build can get its own hat, face and scarf. This guide is written for Wonderfall 1.6.1, on the Mac App Store since November 23, 2026 (checked November 26, 2026): how to start, how to build a snowman, how to dress it, how to put it on a window and how to clean up afterwards.

How do you make snow pile up on your Mac screen?

Turn on Snow accumulation in Wonderfall’s Settings, and snow settles along the bottom of the screen into a bank you can play with.

  1. Install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store and choose Snow in the Season menu.
  2. Open Settings, go to Surface and tools and, under More effects, turn on Snow accumulation. The weather area switches to Full screen, and snow starts to settle along the bottom of the display.
  3. Give it a little time. The more it snows, the deeper the bank gets. A higher intensity fills it faster.
  4. Choose Play in the snow in the menu bar, click the mitten on the tool rack, or press Control-Option-P. Press Esc or choose Stop playing when you are done.

On a fresh install, Snow accumulation is already on and a six-step tour walks you through it: open the menu, “Pick up the mitten”, “Roll a snowball”, “Build a snowman”, “Throw a snowball” and “Dress your snowman”. It has Skip and Next buttons, and each step moves on by itself once you do it. The tour appears only on a fresh install in the snow season and can’t be replayed. After that, short hints appear where you need them, such as “Right-click to dress it” over a new snowman. To see them again, click Show again next to Snow play tips in Settings > Surface and tools.

How do you roll a snowball?

Press on the snow and drag sideways along the bank: the ball picks up snow as it rolls and grows.

In snow play the pointer turns into a mitten, and since version 1.6 rolling and lifting snowballs is more reliable.

How do you build a snowman?

Stack three balls from largest to smallest: a big one for the base, a medium one for the body and a small one for the head.

Drop each ball onto a bigger one. When the third ball lands, the snowman gets its outfit: a beanie and a scarf in a random color, or the default outfit you saved in the Snowman Wardrobe.

How the snowman looks depends on Character style in Settings > General: 3D gives it the new 3D artwork, and Pixel keeps the pixel art. New installs start with 3D; if you updated from 1.6, you keep Pixel until you switch.

How do you dress up your snowman?

Right-click or Control-click a finished snowman to open the Snowman Wardrobe, then pick a hat, a face, buttons and clothes; every change shows on the snowman right away.

The wardrobe arrived in Wonderfall 1.6. It opens as a window with a preview and four tabs:

  • Hat: Beanie, Santa Hat, Top Hat, Bucket Hat, Trapper Hat, Beret, Earmuffs or None. Santa Hat arrived in version 1.6.1.
  • Face: Smile, Surprised, Sleepy, Wink, Laugh or Shy.
  • Body: Coal Buttons, Wooden Buttons, Stars, Red Toggles, Pine Badge or None.
  • Clothes: Red Scarf, Bow Tie, Green Scarf, Plaid Scarf, Winter Cape, Neck Warmer or None.

Can’t decide? Randomize Outfit picks a combination for you. Found a look you like? Make Default saves it, and every new snowman you build wears it. Since version 1.6.1 the wardrobe opens only from the snowman: Settings > Surface and tools > Snow play keeps a short Snowman Wardrobe row, and its Reset button, shown only while a default outfit is set, brings back the automatic look: a beanie and a scarf in a random color. The clothes come in fixed colors; there is no color picker for them.

Can your snowman stand on top of a window?

Since Wonderfall 1.6.1, a finished snowman can stand on the top edge of an app window and ride along when you move the window.

  1. Build a snowman in snow play.
  2. Lift the finished snowman with the mitten.
  3. Drop it on the top edge of an ordinary app window, such as Finder or your browser.

Only the bottom ball touches the window. If the window closes or gets covered, the snowman falls back into the snow, and two snowmen can’t share one spot. Christmas trees can ride on windows too: drag a tree onto a window’s top edge. The Christmas tree guide covers the tree itself. Penguins don’t stand on windows.

How do you throw a snowball?

Grab a ball, flick it up quickly and let go.

Snowballs splat against the edges of the screen and the edges of your windows, and a good hit can knock a snowman over.

Does snow settle on your windows too?

Yes. With Snow accumulation on and the Full screen weather area, snow also gathers on the top edges of your open windows and moves with them.

Drag a window quickly and the snow slides off. Wonderfall reads only the position of windows for this, so it does not need screen recording permission, and very small windows are ignored. Leaf piles and petal piles settle on windows the same way, and rain splashes on their edges.

Can penguins slide across the same snow?

Yes, since version 1.6: choose Call Penguins in the menu bar menu, and three penguins run in, belly-slide across the snow bank, brake, stand up, wave and run off.

They come only when you call them and leave by themselves, and with the Dock at the bottom of the screen they step up onto it. More about penguins and the other 1.6 additions.

How do you clear the snow?

Pick up the Shovel and drag across the bank to dig it out by hand, or choose Cleanup time! and a small snowplow drives across the screen and clears it for you.

The shovel is in the menu bar menu and on the tool rack at the bottom of the screen, or press Control-Option-S; press Esc to put it back. Since version 1.5 you can choose which vehicle clears the snow in Settings > Surface and tools > Cleaning vehicles: the classic tractor-style snowplow or one of three other styles. In autumn the same command sends a leaf vehicle for the leaves.

Common questions

Which version do I need?

Snow play and snow on windows arrived in Wonderfall 1.3, the choice of cleaning vehicles in 1.5, and the Snowman Wardrobe and penguins in 1.6. Snowmen on windows, the 3D look and the Santa Hat came in 1.6.1. Snow accumulation and the shovel are available from 1.2.

Why don’t I see Play in the snow in the menu?

It appears only when Snow is the current season, Snow accumulation is on, the weather area is Full screen and the Mac runs Wonderfall’s Metal renderer. On a Mac without Metal the snow still falls, but the snow bank and the play mode are hidden.

Will the snowman get in the way of my work?

No. Outside play mode, clicks pass through the snow to whatever is underneath; only a click on the snowman itself opens its wardrobe. You can also put the weather behind your windows with the Desktop only layer.

Can I pick any color for the scarf?

No. Each item in the wardrobe has its own fixed colors, such as the Red Scarf, the Green Scarf or the Plaid Scarf. Only the automatic look picks a random scarf color.

Can I add Christmas lights and a tree too?

Yes. Lights, the tree and snow are independent. Here is how to hang lights along the menu bar, and here is how to put a Christmas tree next to your snowman.

Where should you start?

Get Wonderfall on the Mac App Store (a one-time US$3.99 in the US), choose Snow, turn on Snow accumulation and give it a little time. Then choose Play in the snow, stack your first snowman and right-click it to try on the Santa Hat.
