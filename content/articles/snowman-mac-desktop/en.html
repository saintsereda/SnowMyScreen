To build a snowman on your Mac desktop, install Wonderfall, choose Snow and turn on Snow accumulation so snow piles up along the bottom of the screen. Then choose Play in the snow in the menu bar: drag along the snow to roll a snowball and stack three balls into a snowman. Since Wonderfall 1.6, right-click the snowman to dress it in the Snowman Wardrobe.

Falling snow is nice to look at. Snow you can play with is better. Wonderfall turns the snow bank at the bottom of your screen into something you can scoop up, roll and build with, and since version 1.6 every snowman you build can get its own hat, face and scarf. This guide is written for Wonderfall 1.6, on the Mac App Store since November 19, 2026: how to start, how to build a snowman that stands, how to dress it and how to clean up afterwards.

How do you make snow pile up on your Mac screen?

Turn on Snow accumulation in Wonderfall’s Settings, and snow settles along the bottom of the screen into a bank you can play with.

  1. Install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store and choose Snow in the Season menu.
  2. Open Settings, go to Surface and tools and, under More effects, turn on Snow accumulation. The weather area switches to Full screen, and snow starts to settle along the bottom of the display.
  3. Wait a minute. The more it snows, the deeper the bank gets. A higher intensity fills it faster.
  4. Choose Play in the snow in the menu bar, or press Control-Option-P. A short tutorial shows the three moves the first time: roll a snowball, build a snowman, throw a snowball. Press Esc or choose Stop playing when you are done.

How do you roll a snowball?

Press on the snow and drag sideways along the bank: the ball picks up snow as it rolls and grows.

In snow play the pointer turns into a mitten, and since version 1.6 rolling and lifting snowballs is more reliable.

How do you build a snowman?

Stack three balls from largest to smallest: a big one for the base, a medium one for the body and a small one for the head.

Drop each ball onto a bigger one. When the third ball lands, the snowman waves hello and gets its outfit: a beanie and a scarf in a random color, or the default outfit you saved in the Snowman Wardrobe.

A few things to know:

  • Carry the base gently. Shake it too hard and the head falls off.
  • You can keep up to three snowmen on the screen at once. Build a fourth, and the oldest one falls apart.
  • A finished snowman can take a few hits: the first snowball knocks its hat off, the next ones rock it, and the fourth tips it over into the snow. Drop the hat back on its owner to put it on again.

How do you dress up your snowman?

Right-click or Control-click a finished snowman to open the Snowman Wardrobe, then pick a hat, a face, buttons and clothes; every change shows on the snowman right away.

The wardrobe arrived in Wonderfall 1.6. It opens as a window with a preview and four tabs:

  • Hat: Beanie, Top Hat, Bucket Hat, Trapper Hat, Beret, Earmuffs or None.
  • Face: Smile, Surprised, Sleepy, Wink, Laugh or Shy.
  • Body: Coal Buttons, Wooden Buttons, Stars, Red Toggles, Pine Badge or None.
  • Clothes: Red Scarf, Bow Tie, Green Scarf, Plaid Scarf, Winter Cape, Neck Warmer or None.

Can’t decide? Randomize Outfit picks a combination for you. Found a look you like? Make Default saves it, and every new snowman you build wears it. You can also set the default in Settings > Surface and tools > Snow play > Snowman Wardrobe, where Reset brings back the automatic look: a beanie and a scarf in a random color. The clothes come in fixed colors; there is no color picker for them.

How do you throw a snowball?

Grab a ball, flick it up quickly and let go.

Snowballs splat against the edges of the screen and the edges of your windows. Hit a snowman four times and it topples into the snow.

Does snow settle on your windows too?

Yes. With Snow accumulation on and the Full screen weather area, snow also gathers on the top edges of your open windows and moves with them.

Drag a window quickly and the snow slides off. Wonderfall reads only the position of windows for this, so it does not need screen recording permission, and very small windows are ignored. Leaf piles and petal piles settle on windows the same way, and rain splashes on their edges.

Can penguins slide across the same snow?

Yes, since version 1.6: choose Call Penguins in the menu bar menu, and three penguins run in, belly-slide across the snow bank, brake, stand up, wave and run off.

They come only when you call them and leave by themselves, and with the Dock at the bottom of the screen they step up onto it. More about penguins and the other 1.6 additions.

How do you clear the snow?

Pick up the Shovel and drag across the bank to dig it out by hand, or choose Cleanup time! and a small snowplow drives across the screen and clears it for you.

The shovel is in the menu bar menu and on the tool rack at the bottom of the screen, or press Control-Option-S; press Esc to put it back. Since version 1.5 you can choose which vehicle clears the snow in Settings > Surface and tools > Cleaning vehicles: the classic tractor-style snowplow or one of three other styles. In autumn the same command sends a leaf vehicle for the leaves.

Common questions

Which version do I need?

Snow play and snow on windows arrived in Wonderfall 1.3, the choice of cleaning vehicles in 1.5, and the Snowman Wardrobe and penguins in 1.6. Snow accumulation and the shovel are available from 1.2.

Why don’t I see Play in the snow in the menu?

It appears only when Snow is the current season, Snow accumulation is on, the weather area is Full screen and the Mac runs Wonderfall’s Metal renderer. On a Mac without Metal the snow still falls, but the snow bank and the play mode are hidden.

Will the snowman get in the way of my work?

No. Outside play mode, clicks pass through the snow to whatever is underneath; the snowman itself only responds to a right-click or Control-click, which opens its wardrobe. You can also put the weather behind your windows with the Desktop only layer.

Can I pick any color for the scarf?

No. Each item in the wardrobe has its own fixed colors, such as the Red Scarf, the Green Scarf or the Plaid Scarf. Only the automatic look picks a random scarf color.

Can I add Christmas lights and a tree too?

Yes. Lights, the tree and snow are independent. Here is how to hang lights along the menu bar, and here is how to put a Christmas tree next to your snowman.

Where should you start?

Get Wonderfall on the Mac App Store (a one-time US$3.99 in the US), choose Snow, turn on Snow accumulation and give it a minute. Then choose Play in the snow, stack your first snowman and right-click it to try on a top hat.
