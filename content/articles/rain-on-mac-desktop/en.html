Rain is the quiet weather. It works for an autumn afternoon, a grey week in January or a focused evening when snow feels too festive. This guide covers how to turn it on, how to make it a light drizzle or a proper shower, and how the raindrops on the glass work.

How to put a rain effect on your Mac desktop

  1. Install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store. It needs macOS 14.6 or later.
  2. Click the Wonderfall icon in the menu bar and choose Rain in the Season menu.
  3. Pick a preset: Drizzle, Steady rain or Shower.
  4. Choose the area. Top strip keeps the rain near the menu bar; Full screen covers the whole display.

Your wallpaper stays as it is. Rain is drawn on a transparent layer, and clicks pass straight through it to your windows and files.

Make it a drizzle or a shower

The three presets set everything at once. For finer control, choose Custom and adjust Intensity, Drop size, Speed and Wind. Wind tilts the rain left or right, so a strong setting gives you slanted, stormy rain and No Wind gives straight, calm lines.

Rain keeps its own settings. If you switch to snow and back, your rain looks the way you left it.

Raindrops on the glass, and a squeegee to wipe them

In Settings, under More effects, turn on Raindrops on screen. Drops start to gather on the screen as if it were a window during a downpour. When you want a clear view, choose Squeegee in the menu and drag across the glass, or use Clear all droplets to wipe everything at once. Press Esc to put the squeegee away.

Raindrops on screen use Wonderfall’s Metal renderer and the Full screen area. Turning the option on switches the area to Full screen for you. On a Mac where Metal is unavailable, the rain still falls, but the glass effect is hidden.

Show rain over your windows or only on the desktop

Over windows keeps the rain visible while you work. Desktop only puts it behind your open windows, like a living wallpaper that appears when you move them aside. Decorative lights have their own layer setting, so you can keep rain behind your windows and the lights on top.

Let it rain when it rains outside

Turn on Match local weather, and Wonderfall uses Apple Weather to pick the effect: rain on rainy and stormy days, snow when it snows. On dry days it picks a seasonal effect instead. Location is requested only when you turn this on, and changing any slider switches matching off so your manual choice wins.

Common questions

Is this a live wallpaper?

No. A live wallpaper replaces your background. Wonderfall keeps your own wallpaper and draws the rain on a separate transparent layer. Here is how an overlay compares with wallpapers and screensavers.

Does it play rain sounds?

No. The rain is visual. Only the cleaning tools, such as the squeegee, make short sounds while you use them.

Can I combine rain with lights?

Yes. Weather and lights are separate. See how to set up the lights.

Does it slow down my Mac?

Wonderfall renders with Metal and lowers its frame rate in Low Power Mode or when the Mac gets hot, then returns to full speed when conditions improve.
