Apple’s wallpaper guide lists no New Year wallpapers, but you can give your Mac an animated New Year desktop without changing your wallpaper. Install Wonderfall, choose the New Year scene in Lights, pick the 2027 digits bulb shape and add gentle snow in Weather. The lights glow along the menu bar and snow falls over your own wallpaper while you work.

Most “New Year live wallpaper” results are video files for phones and PCs. Wonderfall works differently: it draws the New Year part on transparent layers above the wallpaper you already have, so you keep your picture and choose everything that moves on top of it. This guide is written for Wonderfall 1.6, on the Mac App Store since November 19, 2026.

Is there an animated New Year wallpaper for Mac?

Not built in: Apple’s wallpaper guide lists no holiday or New Year wallpapers, and a screen saver only starts when your Mac sits idle.

How do you make your Mac desktop look like New Year’s Eve?

It takes six steps in Wonderfall, and your wallpaper stays as it is.

  1. Install Wonderfall from the Mac App Store. It needs macOS 14.6 or later and runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
  2. Open Settings. Click the Wonderfall icon in the menu bar and choose Settings…
  3. Turn on the lights. In Lights, turn on Light Strip, then click New Year under Scenes.
  4. Add the year. Under Bulb shape, choose 2027 digits.
  5. Add snow. In Weather, choose Snow and the Gentle preset.
  6. Choose the layer. Weather and Lights each have a Display layer: Over windows keeps them in view while you work, and Desktop only keeps them behind your windows like part of the wallpaper.

You can also switch layers without opening Settings, from the Weather display and Lights display submenus in the Wonderfall menu.

How do you set up New Year lights with 2027 bulbs?

The New Year scene sets the palette, bulb shape and animation in one click, and you can change any of them afterwards.

The string hangs along the menu bar, with 12 palettes, 20 bulb shapes and 10 animations to choose from. The shapes include a star, a gift, a pine tree and the 2027 digits, where the bulbs are the digits 2, 0, 2 and 7. The animations include Twinkle, Sparkle, Wave and Chase. You can also set the color order, bulb size (8 to 20 pt), spacing (24 to 72 pt), brightness and glow. Since version 1.5, Use custom colors lets you pick one to six colors of your own with the standard macOS color picker.

Hide lights on hover is on by default, so the bulbs fade when the pointer is over them. With Reduce motion turned on in System Settings > Accessibility > Motion, the lights glow steadily instead of animating. The lights run along the menu bar only, not around the screen edge or on the Dock. The Christmas lights guide covers every option.

How do you add snow to a New Year desktop?

In Weather, choose Snow and the Gentle preset: light snow falls over your wallpaper without drawing attention from the lights.

For heavier snow, choose Balanced or Intense, or Custom to set Intensity, Flake size, Speed and Wind yourself. There are ten built-in flake shapes; use one or mix several. Weather area decides where it falls: Top strip keeps the snow near the menu bar under the lights, and Full screen covers the whole display. For drifts along the bottom, turn on Snow accumulation in Surface and tools; that needs the Full screen area and a Mac that runs Wonderfall’s Metal renderer. The snow guide explains every control.

Can the lights switch to New Year by themselves?

Yes, since version 1.5. Turn on Switch light styles for holidays in Lights, and from December 27 to January 1 the lights change to the Midnight Gold palette with star bulbs.

Dates follow your Mac’s local time, first and last day included. Before that, from December 1 to 26, the Christmas style uses the Velvet Holiday palette with gift bulbs. Each holiday can be turned on or off separately. Only the palette and bulb shape change; your animation, brightness, size and spacing stay, and your own palette and shape come back when the holiday ends.

One thing to know: the 2027 digits and Midnight Gold’s stars don’t show at the same time, because the holiday style sets its own bulb shape. If you want the digits on New Year’s Eve, turn off the holiday style for New Year only (each holiday has its own switch), or choose 2027 digits again after December 27: changing the scene, palette or shape during a holiday pauses the holiday style until that holiday is over. The scenes themselves never switch on a schedule; only the holiday styles follow the calendar.

What else can you put on a New Year desktop?

A Christmas tree, Santa, a snowman and penguins: they all come with the same app and sit well under New Year lights.

  • The tree. In Settings it is called Christmas tree and lives in Holiday, but it stays until you turn it off, so it suits New Year just as well. You can decorate it and, since 1.6, place more trees from Decoration Inventory. The Christmas tree guide shows how.
  • Santa. In Holiday, set Santa flyover to Sometimes (roughly every 10 to 25 minutes) or Often (roughly every 3 to 8 minutes). Call Santa now sends him right away, at midnight if you like. Santa doesn’t fly while Reduce motion is on.
  • A snowman. Turn on Snow accumulation with the Full screen weather area, choose Play in the snow, roll three balls and stack them. How to build a snowman on your Mac desktop.
  • Penguins. Choose Call Penguins in the Wonderfall menu, and three penguins run in, belly-slide across the snow, wave and run off.

Can your own New Year images fall instead of snow?

Yes. Choose Your image and add up to ten pictures of your own, such as stars, confetti or a “2027”, and they fall instead of snowflakes.

Add them with the file picker or by dragging them into the gallery; common image formats and SVG work, and Wonderfall prepares each one automatically, keeping transparency. Pictures with transparent backgrounds look best. Motion style sets how they move: Like snow, Like leaves or Like rain, and Shuffle all randomly mixes several. Only one effect runs at a time, so it is either snowflakes or your images. Your images stay on your Mac.

What doesn’t Wonderfall do for New Year?

Wonderfall has no countdown to midnight, no fireworks and no New Year wallpapers.

  • No countdown. The New Year scene’s subtitle reads “A bright countdown”, but that describes the mood; there is no timer in the app.
  • No fireworks. The effects are snow, rain, autumn leaves, spring petals, summer fireflies and your own images.
  • No wallpapers. Wonderfall doesn’t install, download or replace wallpapers; the picture underneath is yours to choose.
  • No music. Only the cleaning tools, snow play and Santa’s sleigh make sounds, and the sleigh sound can be turned off.
  • Not a screen saver. It doesn’t replace the macOS screen saver; it works on your desktop while you use your Mac.

What do you do with a New Year desktop after the holidays?

Very little: the holiday light style switches back to your own settings by itself after January 1, and the rest takes a minute.

If you set the 2027 digits by hand, pick another bulb shape or switch to the Winter Night scene to carry winter on. The same Christmas tree item in the Wonderfall menu turns the tree off, and extra trees from the inventory go away with a right-click and Return to Inventory. The snow can stay as long as you like. Common questions

Will Wonderfall replace my wallpaper?

No. It draws the lights and snow on transparent layers above your wallpaper. Turn the effects off, and your desktop is exactly as it was.

Will the lights and snow get in the way of my work?

No. Clicks pass through to your apps and desktop icons. If the snow distracts you, set the weather layer to Desktop only and it stays behind your windows.

Is there a New Year countdown?

No, Wonderfall has no timer. You can still call Santa at midnight with Call Santa now.

Does it work on a MacBook Air?

Yes, if it runs macOS 14.6 or later: Wonderfall runs on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Wonderfall lowers its frame rate from 60 to 30 frames per second in Low Power Mode, on Intel Macs and when the Mac is under heavy thermal load.

Where should you start?

Get Wonderfall on the Mac App Store (a one-time US$3.99 in the US), turn on the New Year scene, the 2027 digits and gentle snow. To skip thinking about dates, turn on Switch light styles for holidays, and your lights dress up for Christmas and New Year by themselves. Happy New Year!
